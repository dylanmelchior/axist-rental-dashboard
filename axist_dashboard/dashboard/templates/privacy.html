Privacy Policy

Internal Dashboard  ·  Effective: June 25, 2026

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Axis T Rentals ("Company") collects, uses, and protects information processed through the Axis T Rentals Internal Dashboard ("the Software"). This Software is intended exclusively for internal use by authorized employees and contractors.

02

Information Collected

The Software may collect and process the following categories of information: (a) employee login credentials and access logs; (b) customer and rental data entered or imported by employees; (c) financial data synchronized via QuickBooks Online integration; (d) usage activity within the Software for audit and security purposes.

03

How Information Is Used

Data collected through the Software is used solely to facilitate internal rental management, financial record-keeping, and business operations. Data is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing purposes, or used outside the scope of Axis T Rentals business operations.

04

Third-Party Data Sharing

The Software integrates with QuickBooks Online. Data transmitted to QuickBooks is governed by Intuit's privacy policy. Axis T Rentals does not share customer or financial data with any other third parties except as required by law or as necessary for business operations (e.g., payment processors).

05

Data Security

Axis T Rentals implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data processed through the Software. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. Employees are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.

06

Data Retention

Data retained through the Software is kept for the duration required by applicable business and legal obligations. Employees may not delete customer or financial records without authorization from management.

07

Employee Privacy

Activity within the Software, including login times, actions performed, and data accessed, may be logged and reviewed by system administrators for security and compliance purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using this Software on Company systems.

08

No End-Consumer Data Collection

This Software does not directly collect data from end consumers or members of the public. All customer data processed through the Software is entered by authorized employees in the course of their duties.

09

Changes to This Policy

Axis T Rentals may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Employees will be notified of material changes. Continued use of the Software following notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to the system administrator at Axis T Rentals, 351 W 400 S, Salt Lake City, UT. You can also reach us at axist.com.
